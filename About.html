Our Story

Ubuntu Youth Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation established in 2018 with a focus on supporting young people from disadvantaged communities.

The foundation was created in response to challenges faced by young people when accessing quality education, digital resources, career guidance and mentorship opportunities.

Our goal is to connect young people with resources, skills and support that can help them become confident and productive members of their communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower young people through education, digital skills, mentorship and community support, helping them develop the knowledge and confidence needed to achieve their goals.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a future where every young person has access to opportunities, resources and support that enable them to reach their full potential.

Who We Help

Ubuntu Youth Foundation primarily supports young people between the ages of 13 and 24, particularly those from disadvantaged communities.

  • High school learners needing academic support
  • Young people seeking digital skills
  • Students preparing for further education
  • Young adults seeking career guidance
  • Young people looking for mentorship and personal development

Our Values
